World of Warcraft UI artwork belongs to Blizzard Entertainment.
The six BLP files are unmodified native interface resources bundled for
use in WoW. The TGA files in Parts are derived from those same six BLP
images, using our own masks to progressively reveal the original artwork.
They contain cumulative stages and complementary individual pieces for
the top and side layouts. No artwork from another addon is included.

TimeFeather.tga is an addon-created geometric alpha mask, independent of
the Blizzard artwork. It feathers the edges of a native duration bar mask.
The addon references Blizzard's native cooldown-manager SoundKit IDs;
no audio recordings or extracted sound files are bundled.
